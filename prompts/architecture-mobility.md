00:00–00:06: Start at normal standing eye level just inside the entry with a 28mm-equivalent lens, no fisheye. Move forward one meter to reveal the kitchenette on the left and closed bathroom door on the right; pause so the narrow clearance is visible.
00:06–00:12: Turn slowly toward the living area. A resident lowers the wall table using both hands and places one chair, demonstrating real walking clearance without removing furniture.
00:12–00:18: Continue to the window, then turn back toward the entry in one continuous movement, showing the full path and sofa-bed depth. Finish on a stable wide frame with no hidden wall.

Audio: room tone, hinge, distant street. Natural morning light and neutral grade. No impossible wide-angle expansion, moving walls, duplicated doors, instant decluttering, fake view, text, logo, or watermark.
