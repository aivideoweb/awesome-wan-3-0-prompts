# Six VideoWeb practice briefs / 六条补充练习

[120 core briefs](README.md) · [X videos](../guides/x-community-showcase.md) · [VideoWeb workflow](../guides/videoweb-workflow.md)

These are new editorial exercises for practicing product geometry, camera direction, character continuity, shot planning and audio. They are **not author quotations, reconstructions, or the prompts behind the linked X videos**. They have not been rendered or validated on VideoWeb. The 120 inherited briefs remain a separate catalog; this page adds six exercises.

以下六条为新增练习，不是 X 作者原文或对应视频的复现提示词，尚未生成验证。请按当前界面选择参数；参考模式和声音需要相应功能支持。

<a id="prompt-01"></a>

## 01 — Product reveal / 商品展示

**Setup:** Text or owned first frame; 8 seconds; 16:9.

```text
A plain turquoise glass bottle stands on dry pale stone beside a window. 0–2 seconds: a fixed close shot establishes its straight edges and round cap. 2–6 seconds: the camera makes one slow 30-degree arc while sunlight reflected from water outside travels across the background. 6–8 seconds: the camera stops with the entire bottle visible. Preserve the bottle silhouette, cap and blank label area; the bottle never rotates. Soft daylight, restrained highlights. Optional audio: quiet room ambience. No writing, logos, extra objects, liquid inside changing level or glass deformation.
```

**First adjustment:** If the bottle bends, remove the arc and try a fixed camera first.

## 02 — Travel memory / 旅行片段

**Setup:** Text; 10 seconds; 16:9.

```text
One continuous travel-documentary shot at a quiet seaside overlook. An adult traveler in a tan jacket with a dark blue backpack stands on the left, looking toward the harbor. 0–3 seconds: hold a medium shot from behind. 3–8 seconds: slowly pan right to reveal fishing boats, retaining the traveler at the edge. 8–10 seconds: settle on the harbor. A light breeze moves only loose fabric and distant flags in one direction. Natural evening light, understated handheld movement. Optional audio: wind and distant boat engines. No dialogue, titles, time jumps, duplicated boats or changing jacket color.
```

**First adjustment:** Keep the traveler visible; if identity drifts, shorten the pan.

<a id="prompt-03"></a>

## 03 — Single-character obstacle / 单角色障碍

**Setup:** Text or owned character frame; 10 seconds; 16:9.

```text
An original small orange woodland fox with a cream muzzle and dark paws walks left to right beside a shallow stream. 0–3 seconds: follow at fox-eye height as it approaches a fallen branch. 3–6 seconds: it stops, lowers its head and sniffs once. 6–8 seconds: it steps over the branch, front paws first and back paws second. 8–10 seconds: it continues walking right. Warm stylized animation with consistent fur shapes. The camera stays on the same side of the stream. Optional audio: water and soft paw steps. Keep exactly one fox with four legs and one tail. No teleporting, speech, clothing or new animals.
```

**First adjustment:** If the step fails, lower the branch before adding more action.

<a id="prompt-04"></a>

## 04 — Three-shot spatial continuity / 三镜头方向

**Setup:** Text; 12 seconds; 16:9; split into separate tasks if needed.

```text
A silent rehearsal in an empty dance studio with a wooden floor and a blue door on the right. One adult dancer wears a gray shirt, black trousers and white shoes. Exactly three shots: 0–4 seconds, wide view as the dancer walks from screen-left toward the blue door; 4–8 seconds, a closer side view from the same side of the room as the dancer pauses and raises the right hand; 8–12 seconds, return to the original wide view as the hand lowers and the dancer stops beside the door. Preserve left-to-right travel, outfit, door position and floor reflections. No crossing the camera axis, duplicate people, changing shoes or extra cuts. Optional audio: footsteps and room tone.
```

**First adjustment:** If shot count fails, generate the three beats separately with the same reference frame.

<a id="prompt-05"></a>

## 05 — Two-person dialogue / 双人对白

**Setup:** Text or owned first frame; 10 seconds; 16:9; sound only if supported.

```text
Fixed medium-wide view of two adult friends seated across a small cafe table. The speaker on screen-left wears an olive sweater; the listener on screen-right wears a cream jacket. 0–2 seconds: both look at the closed notebook on the table. 2–5 seconds: only the left person says in natural Mandarin, “明天我们早点出发。” 5–7 seconds: a quiet pause; the right person nods once with mouth closed. 7–10 seconds: both smile gently. Soft window light, stable eyelines. Keep faces, seating, clothes, cups and notebook fixed. No subtitles, simultaneous speech, lip motion from the listener, camera cuts or extra hands.
```

**First adjustment:** If audio fails, keep the visual take and record the one spoken line separately.

<a id="prompt-06"></a>

## 06 — Synchronized action sound / 动作与声音

**Setup:** Text; 8 seconds; 1:1; sound only if supported.

```text
A close tabletop shot of one adult hand placing a plain wooden block onto a felt mat. 0–2 seconds: the hand holds the block still above the mat. 2–4 seconds: lower it in a straight line until contact. At contact, one soft dull tap, synchronized with the visible landing. 4–6 seconds: the fingers release, then retreat upward. 6–8 seconds: hold the block perfectly still. Locked camera, soft sidelight, natural wood grain and accurate shadows. Keep five fingers, one block and one mat. No bounce, duplicate impacts, music, dialogue, writing, camera movement or object deformation.
```

**First adjustment:** If contact and sound drift apart, simplify to one impact and remove all other sound cues.
