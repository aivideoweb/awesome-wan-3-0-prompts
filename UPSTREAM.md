## Editorial corrections to inherited material

On 2026-09-22, this edition revised two core briefs: [sign-language evacuation briefing](prompts/people-dialogue-localization.md#prompt-03) and [signed science demonstration](prompts/education-science.md#prompt-09). Both now require users to supply a target sign language and regional variety, approved message content and authorized human-recorded signing for post-production. The science brief supplies draft English meanings for a qualified signer to translate and review, not a pre-approved signed message. Both distinguish layout rehearsal from a reviewed final video. They remain untested production templates; the remaining 118 core prompt bodies are unchanged from the pinned source. Downloads include the revised text.

The [writing guide](guides/prompting-guide.md) corrects how unsupported parameters are handled. The [retry guide](guides/troubleshooting.md) replaces a context-dependent instruction with a full-prompt editing method. These are local editorial changes, not new claims about model capabilities.
